## 1. Ethical Compliance and Data Governance Protocol

All speech recordings in this corpus were collected under strict adherence to academic research ethics and the IIT Gandhinagar Student Honour Code:

1. **Informed Consent**: Every participant was briefed regarding the research objective (evaluating automatic speech recognition, code-mixing dynamics, and acoustic distribution drift) prior to recording.
2. **Voluntary Participation**: Participation was entirely voluntary with zero academic coercion or financial incentives. Speakers were given the explicit right to withdraw their recordings at any point prior to dataset freezing.
3. **Strict Anonymization**: No personally identifiable information (PII) such as student roll numbers, email addresses, phone numbers, or government IDs are stored in the data repository. All records are indexed solely by pseudonymized alphanumeric identifiers (`P1` through `P19`).
4. **Permissible Use Scope**: Audio and transcripts are authorized exclusively for non-commercial academic research, machine learning benchmark evaluation, and ASR model fine-tuning (Assignment 1 and Assignment 2 of CS203).
